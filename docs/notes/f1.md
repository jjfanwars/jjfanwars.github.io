# James Racing (`f1/`): notes for Claude

Read `CLAUDE.md` first (shared rules). Split out of it on 17 Nov 2026; keep this file up to date when you change the game.

- **James Racing (`f1/index.html`, live at /f1; `rally/f1.html` redirects there; 16 Nov 2026):** realistic first-person F1, linked from the Rally menu (`#bF1`). Original circuits, teams and drivers (no real F1 names or liveries). Own renderer, not the rally engine. The page loads its scripts from `../rally/js/`. Four files:
  - `js/f1gfx.js` (`JRG`): procedural PBR textures (`textures()`: asphalt with normal and roughness maps, grass, gravel, carbon weave, armco, tree cards, contact-shadow `ao`). The car is built once as shared geometry (`carGeometry`: `loft` from cross-section keys for the nose/tub, sidepods and engine cover; `wing` NACA airfoils; `plate`; `rod` suspension; merged per material by `merge`), with per-team materials (`livery` canvas: u runs along the car, v around the section; clearcoat paint). Tyres are a `LatheGeometry` with sidewall print (`tyreTex`), the wheel covers swap to a blurred texture at speed, and the rear light flashes while harvesting (`updateCar`). `buildCockpit`: an extruded steering wheel with buttons, rotaries and paddles, gloved `hand`s (finger capsules wrapped round the grips, thumbs on the face), arms in the race suit solved by two-bone IK each frame (`update`), and mirrors fed by a HalfFloat render target. `skyMaterial` (fbm clouds, sun glare), `envMap` (PMREM of the sky, `scene.environment`). `Post`: MSAA HalfFloat target, then bright pass, blur, and a composite pass (speed blur, chromatic edges, ACES, sRGB, vignette, grain); used only on High graphics, while Low renders straight to the screen with ACES. `FX`: sparks (additive line segments) and tyre smoke sprites.
  - `js/f1track.js` (`F1T`): `CIRCUITS` (Kestrel Park, Marina Bay Loop: clockwise control points, closed centripetal Catmull-Rom resampled every 2 m), `build(ci)` gives `X/Z/H/K` (curvature, + = right), `KS` (smoothed |K|), `kerb`, `gravel` (1 = left side, 2 = right), barrier distances `barL/barR` (clamped so walls never cross another part of the track), `pit` (left of the main straight), `query(x, z, hint)` gives nearest index, lateral `d` (+ right) and `s`, `surf(i, d)` (0 tarmac, 1 kerb, 2 grass, 3 gravel, 4 run-off), a racing line `L` (elastic band toward minimum curvature, `LX/LZ/LK`), `profile(gripK)` (speed profile, `V`), braking points `brakes`, `drs` zones (longest straights, detection 60 m before), `AERO` (mass 880, ClA 5, CdA 1.3, mu 1.75).
  - Physics update (16 Nov, later): lateral tyre forces are per wheel (`Z` loads with lateral transfer, front roll share `RSF`, load sensitivity `lsw`, Ackermann, per-wheel friction circle, longitudinal split by capacity plus an `LSD` bias); steering lock `F1.LOCK` 0.38 rad (use it, not 0.34); the speed profile uses 0.95 x mu for the transfer loss. Wear about 0.09 per lap on mediums (S x1.7, H x0.55) with a grip cliff past 0.7.
  - Pit stops (in `f1.js`): pit lane from `T.pitS0` (-220 m) to `T.pitS1` (+260 m) on the left (`T.lane`; the pit wall `T.pit` leaves 60 m entry/exit gaps). `c.wantPit` + crossing the entry hands the car to `pitStep` (kinematic: `pitLat`/`pitPose`, limiter `PIT_LIM`, boxes `boxU(team)`, 2.1-2.8 s stop, sometimes a slow one, +4.5 s for a wing; new tyres cold at 72 C; `setTyreLook`). Pit cars are skipped by `collide`, `wakes` and the AI. `aiStrategy` (planned stop in 10+ lap races, `G.mandatory` two compounds or +20 s, dead tyres, wing damage). Crews per team (`buildCrews`, `JRG.person`, `crewFrame`: jacks lift the car, wheels off briefly). Keys B / 1-3. Team radio `radio()` (+ optional speechSynthesis, setting `voice`), `lapRadio`. Settings `input` (mouse steering: x position steers, LMB throttle, RMB brake), `visor` (CSS `#visor` helmet frame in the cockpit view). Wheel: physical LEDs `CK.leds`, `CK.flags`, animated `CK.paddles`, limiter page in `drawWheel`.
  - `js/f1car.js` (`F1`): `Car` (planar two-axle model at 240 Hz substeps: Pacejka-style tyres `pac`, wider rears x1.1 for stability at the limit, load transfer, downforce/drag with DRS, `tow` (slipstream -20% drag, dirty air -12% downforce), per-axle lock (`lockF/lockR`) and wheelspin (`spinR`) states, friction circle, ABS/TC that leave lateral grip, floor yaw damping, 8 gears `GEARS`, `icePower` + ERS (`ers` J, modes 0 harvest / 1 balanced / 2 overtake, MGU-H recharge on full throttle), tyre temp `tf/tr` and `wear` with compounds `COMP` S/M/H, `barrier` bounces and wing damage, reverse by holding the brake at a stop), `aiDrive` (pure pursuit on the line plus yaw-rate feedback, target speed from the profile, passes only on straights, side-by-side yields in corners, traction/brake limits by slip and lateral g, reset when stuck), `collide` (three discs per car), `wakes`, `reset`.
  - `js/f1.js`: menu settings in `jr-set`, best laps `jr-best-<circuit>`. Track meshes from `band` (flat non-indexed strips with vertex colours) and `wall`; the ground is a subdivided plane at y -0.12 (one big plane z-fought the track with the log depth buffer). Cars and cockpit come from `JRG` (wheel display drawn by `drawWheel`, mirrors rendered every other frame). Barriers: textured armco + posts, fence, TecPro at gravel traps, ad boards (fictional brands) on fast straights, marshal huts; `wall()` takes `uvS` as a number or `[u, v, y0]`. Trees are instanced alpha cards with colour variation. `draw()` picks the post path; `G.camOv = {p, l}` overrides the camera for screenshots. Views: 0 cockpit (head moves with g-forces, looks into turns), 1 T-cam, 2 chase (V). Race flow in `tick` (120 Hz): `lights` -> `race` -> `done` (player finishes, the AI drives your cool-down lap, results via `renderResults`), `laps` (lap/sector timing, `marks` every 20 m for gaps and DRS 1 s detection), track limits (`me.lim` = 4 wheels past the line: 3 warnings then +5 s; TT deletes the lap), jump start +5 s. `Snd` is a WebAudio synth (V6 harmonics, turbo/MGU-K whine, squeal, kerbs, gravel, two Doppler voices for the nearest rivals). Input: keyboard (ramped pedals, speed-aware steering limit when "Steering help" is on) or gamepad. Test hook `?test` -> `window.__JR` (`start`, `tick`, `G`, `SET`); set `G.me.ai = true` to let the AI drive you. Headless physics tests: load util/f1track/f1car into a Node `vm` and step `Car`s directly. AI pace and balance were only tuned headless (Pro about 66 s at Kestrel, 68 s at Marina). Steering input (`readInput`, 16 Nov 2026): `lim` (Steering help, speed-aware lock) applies to keyboard, mouse and pad; keyboard ramps `ctl.raw` with a hold-time rate (0.7 + 3.2 x held, max 2.3, centring 2.8, reversing 3.6, all x max(lim, 0.3)), mouse is rate-limited to 6/s, then every input goes through `smooth` (exponential, k 10 keyboard / 12 mouse / 14 pad) into `ctl.st`.
