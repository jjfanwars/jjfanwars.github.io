# jjdrop (`drop.html`): notes for Claude

Read `CLAUDE.md` first (shared rules). Split out of it on 17 Nov 2026; keep this file up to date when you change the game.

- **jjdrop (`drop.html`, 11 Nov 2026, reworked as urban CQB, then multi-storey + online + Siege):** separate first-person urban combat game in body-cam style, live at /drop.html, linked from jjfront (menu button, in-game `#dropBtn` passes `?squad=&foe=`; `?mode=siege` also works). Design notes and the genre comparison (R6 Siege, CS, Insurgency, Bodycam, Ready or Not) are in `docs/plans/drop.md`. Loads the Three.js r170 classic build shared as `three-r170.js` (do not edit). **Source layout:** one file; the code was assembled from parts, so there is no build step, just edit `drop.html`. Test hook: open with `?test` for `window.__D`; headless Chromium needs `--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`; for logic-only tests stub `requestAnimationFrame` (`addInitScript`) and call `__D.step(1/30)` in a loop, it runs thousands of steps per second that way.
  - **World:** 3x3 city blocks of 76 m on a 96 m pitch, 20 m streets (`BC`, `RDS`, `HALF` 154), perimeter wall. `building()` makes real storeys: up to `MAXF` 4 enterable (`nf`, default 3, Bravo 4, Alpha 1), slabs with stairwell holes (`hole<k>`), a corner stair core (two columns, switchback, `STAIRS`, steps are solids), partitions that avoid the core with a door in every stretch (`segDoors`), exterior doors shifted away from partition ends. Floors above `nf` are a visual mass. Furniture from `furn()`. Geometry is merged per material (`addBox`, `texMat` + `flatMat`); shadows baked (`needsUpdate` after `buildWorld` and after a breach). `SOLIDS` `[x0,x1,z0,z1,top,bottom]` in an 8 m hash `SG` (`blocked`, `hitSolid`, `los`, `standY` steps up to 1.2 m, `pushOut`). Fall damage above about 3 m.
  - **Dynamic pieces:** door leaves (`DLEAF`, open on proximity, `stepDoors`, `setLeaf`; `navRect` restores each leaf to its `d.o` state), siege wall panels (`PANEL`, 2.4 m chunks of the lower 2.4 m, soft or hard, built only when `o.siege`, solids carry `.pan`; intact panels share one merged textured mesh `PANM` rebuilt by `panRebuild` when `panDirty`), openings (`OPEN`) for barricades (`BAR`, plank groups, `barAdd`/`barBreak`, solids carry `.bar`), `reinforce` (steel plate group), `breachPanel`, `melee`.
  - **Destruction:** a soft panel that takes damage gets a cell grid (`CW` 0.2 m, `p.cells`, `p.nc`/`p.nr`); `panHit(p, x, y, z, rad)` removes cells (rad 0 = the one cell), `cellsGone` queues a rebuild (`cellQ`, `panMesh` merges runs of cells, cut edges darker via `addBox(..., cs, ax)`), sends `cl` events, sets `p.open` when a gap 3 cells wide and 1.8 m tall exists (nav then ignores the panel) and breaches the panel under 10% left. `blocked`/`hitSolid` let points through missing cells, `pushOut` lets bodies through via `panGap` (slides toward a nearby gap). Bullets go through up to two soft walls or barricades (x0.62 damage each, `W.cell` chance to knock out a cell), shotgun pellets chew holes, melee and the hammer punch big ones, explosions use `blastWalls`. Debris `chunks`.
  - **Navigation:** 1 m grid with `NL` layers (one per storey), `NAV`, `LINKS` for stairs (each link carries `via` points on the stair centreline), `findPath(sx,sz,sk,gx,gz,gl)` returns `[x,z,layer]` points, `layerOf`, `flood` (building points `b.pts` are only reachable ones), `navRect` after a breach.
  - **Factions and operators:** two fixed teams, `FAC[0]` COMMIES (dark red) and `FAC[1]` FASCISTS (dark blue), which swap attack/defend every round (menu `sTeam` picks yours; host's team from it, guests alternate). `OPS` are political caricatures (`fac` 0: STALIN, PUTIN, KIM, XI, CASTRO; `fac` 1: TRUMP, YAHU, BORIS, THATCHER, ELON); each has `a` (attack kit) and `d` (defend kit) as `[gadget, joke name, count]` plus secondaries `as`/`ds`, every gadget type appears once per faction. `loadout(e)` picks the kit for the current side into `e.uni/e.gu/e.un` and `e.sec/e.gs/e.sn`; all gadget code reads those, not the op. Keep gadget names as bare names (the owner asked for no explanations of the jokes). Satire stays on policy/persona, never ethnicity or religion; no Hitler or Nazi symbols. Looks: `'op<k>'` and `'fac<t>'` looks use `suit.glb` (Mixamo X Bot skeleton + idle/walk/run clips from the three.js examples; its own mesh is hidden) dressed by `dressSuit` in rigid tailored pieces hung on the bones (`seg`: jacket in the team colour, trousers, shirt + tie in the team colour `TIE[fac]`, caps also team-coloured, or closed tunic, skirt for Thatcher, cuffs, hands, shoes; collar style `SUIT_K`); the suit model faces +z so it is not rotated. Heads: `photoHead(st)` (sculpted busts in `heads/<hd>.bin` for the leaders in `BUSTS` (trump, boris, xi, stalin, elon, kim, castro): the sculpt is the shape, the face is painted at runtime on a canvas by `bustTex` (skin shading, hairline, eyes, brows, nostrils, lips, plus `beard`/`stub`/`mo`/`glasses`/`cap`/`shaved` extras; all positions in head-space metres from the `BUSTS` entry) and mapped from the front (`BX0`/`BXW`/`BY0`/`BYH`), hair, cap and back of the head by vertex colour; Castro also gets the modelled cigar; regenerate the bins with the scratch fit script: orient, landmark eyes/chin from a front render, scale eye-chin to 0.11 m, trim at the neck, decimate to 16k tris), otherwise the Lee Perry-Smith head scan, CC BY 3.0 from the three.js examples, trimmed at the neck and baked into head space in `head.bin` (all head bins: eyes y 0.12, chin 0.01, nose tip z 0.11, loaded by `loadHead`), loaded into `HEADB`; `suitReady` waits for all heads and suit.glb; front planar uv from `tex/faces/<hd>.jpg`, back of the head uses the white corner pixel with `FACE_SKIN`) merged with modelled hair/caps/props from `headParts` in `headGeom`, material `faceMat`; hung on the Neck bone, lowered 0.072 m for the suit skeleton, original head bone scaled to 0.001 (`rig.hideHead`), face kept level with the aim each frame (`rig.headM`). Face photos are Wikimedia Commons crops (credits and licences in `tex/faces/LICENSE.txt`; to redo one: crop around an OpenCV Haar face box x1.3, elliptical feather into the median skin colour, 256 px, white 10 px top-left corner). Assault soldiers still use the armoured `soldier.glb`. Bots take their operator's name; first-person sleeves use `FAC[t].sl`. `WPN` (ar, smg, dmr, sg, lmg: damage, rate, mag, spread, kick, pellets, `cell` chance, model, scale `sc` around the grip, sound). `loadout(e)` applies op + weapon. `GADGET` names. First person: `gunG` holds the rifle (`RIFLE.fp`) or the CC0 shotgun (`shotgun.bin`, `SGUN`, loaded with `loadBin`) plus the optic; `setViewWeapon` swaps model, scale, muzzle flash and the left arm (`buildArms(dy, dz)`); `sightH` gives the ADS sight height per weapon and optic. Third-person guns follow `e.w` in `poseSoldier`.
  - **Gadgets:** host-owned objects in `GOBJ` (`addG`/`delG`, events `go`/`gx`): building cameras `cam` (8 per round, `beginPrep`), `scam`, `shock` (on a panel, `p.shock`, thermal fizzles), `laser` (on an `OPEN`, triggers in `gadgetStep`), `jam` (holds charges in `stepCharges`, kills drones), `armor` (plates `e.arm`, absorb half the damage, not on headshots), `wire` (`inWire` slows attackers), `shield` (solid + `navRect`), `nitro`. Thrown/fired ones are `PROJ` (`throwP`, `stepProj`, sticky kinds in `STICK`, events `pr`/`px`), effects in `explode` (host) + `fxExplode` (all, event `fx`): `EXP` table, flash (`blindMe`, `e.blind`), EMP (`ELEC` kinds), smoke (`SMOKES` sprites, `smokeBlocks` for bot sight). Charges (`placeCharge(e, o, d, 'charge'|'therm')`, `addCharge`, `detonate`, events `chg`/`chx`). Pings (`pingAct`, `PINGS`, middle click). Drones are ground drones (`droneStep`, `droneFloor`, `e.drone`, `G.droneOn`, key 5, 2 per round, prep drone is one of them, jammers and bullets kill them); defenders watch `camList` with 5 (`G.camOn`, `setCam`, A/D). All gadget keys go through `useAct(e, kind, eye, dir)` on the host (`use` = E tap, `uni` = G, `sec` = 4, `mel` = B, `png` = drone/camera click, `pg` = middle-click ping, `med` = H for guests).
  - **Models:** articulated soldiers merged per rigid segment (`soldierTpl`, `makeSoldier(look)`, `animSoldier`, `LOOKS`: ally, foe, foe2, att, def), shared geometry per look.
  - **Modes:** `MODE` `'coop'` (Assault: tickets, three buildings captured by being inside on any floor, AI squad follows a human leader, reinforcements) or `'siege'` (teams A/B of `size`, bots fill, `G.rs` = round state; phases `pick` 12 s (operator select overlay `#opsel`, keys 1-5, `hostPick`, bots pick in the last 4 s, `freeOp`), `prep` 40 s (`beginPrep`: loadouts, defuser carrier, cameras, `botFortify` for defender bots), `act` 180 s, `end` 7 s; first to 3 of 5 rounds, roles swap each round, no respawn, spectate teammates). Objective `G.obj` / `cfg.obj`: `'bomb'` (default: `rs.sites` A and B on one floor, `rs.dz` defuser carried/dropped/planted, hold E 7 s to plant on a site (`bombBusy`, `stepBomb`), 45 s timer, defenders hold E 7 s to disable, sites revealed to attackers when a drone sees one or at action) or `'secure'` (hold the objective room 7 s, `rs.obj`). Bots: `attGoal` (carrier plants, others escort, pick up a dropped defuser, guard the plant, `botGadgets` for grenades, smoke on the planter, EMP on electronics, breach plans `e.gplan` for thermal/charges/breaching rounds) and `defGoal` (posts near sites, go disable). `beginMatch(cfg, roster, me, role)` sets up solo, host or guest.
  - **Online:** relay unchanged, rooms named `[Drop] ...`. Host runs everything; guests send `pose` (20 Hz, ignored unless `sp` matches the host's spawn counter; carries `hE` = holding E and `dr` = own drone), `fire` (host fires the weapon's pellets), `use`, `pick`, `ord`; host broadcasts `snap` at 15 Hz (entities with op, kills, deaths, gadget counts, armor, drone; round state with sites and defuser; event list `evq`: sh, tk, dmg, cr, say, feed, kf, bn, bar, rf, brk, cl, chg, chx, zp, go, gx, pr, px, fx, fb, pg, dk, rnd, end). World geometry is regenerated from the shared seed on every client, so anything random in `buildWorld`/`building` must use the seeded `rr` only. No late join, no host migration (a leaver becomes a bot).
  - **Graphics (Siege-like, no body-cam look):** PBR materials (`MeshStandardMaterial` for walls `texMat`, floors `floorMat`, props `flatMat`, soldiers), procedural plaster, hardwood and bump textures, vertex-colour AO gradient in `addBox`, floors in their own merged buffer `FT`, glowing ceiling lamps in `LM`; `LAMPS` are lit by the 5 nearest real point lights (`lampLights`, updated in `gfxUpdate` on the same storey only), hemisphere + baked sun shadows outdoors, sky dome shader (`sky`), haze fog, post pass with bloom (threshold 1.6), ACES tone mapping, cool-shadow/warm-light grade, light vignette and grain, tiny lens distortion. `decalAt` bullet holes (150-mesh pool, `clearDecals` on new match/round), floating dust (`dust`). REC and timestamp overlays are hidden by CSS. Software rendering (swiftshader) needs over a minute to compile the first frame, use a long screenshot timeout.
  - **Assets:** `soldier.glb` is the Mixamo "Vanguard" character with Idle/Walk/Run clips (from the three.js examples, free to use in a game under Mixamo's terms, not CC0, do not offer it as a standalone download); tinted per look (`SOLDIER_TINT`), animated with `AnimationMixer`, spine pitch and two-bone arm IK onto the shared M4A1 mesh (`poseSoldier`, `ik2`, `boneAim`), legs bend for crouch; box soldiers (`makeSoldierOld`) are the fallback until it loads. `gltf-loader.js` is GLTFLoader + SkeletonUtils bundled with esbuild against the shared `window.THREE` (entry imports them from three@0.170.0, a plugin maps `three` to a shim that re-exports `window.THREE`). `tex/*.jpg` are CC0 ambientCG PBR sets (walls PaintedPlaster017, floors WoodFloor051, ground Asphalt031, props Concrete034), see `tex/LICENSE.txt`. First-person arms are built in the `view` group in gun space (`limbMesh`, `boxAt`), sleeves from the screen corners to gloved hands on the grip and handguard.
  - **Optics:** `OPTICS` (iron, red dot, holographic, 2.5x scope, 4x ACOG), menu select `sOptic` and key T, saved in localStorage `jjd-optic`. Each has a mounted model (`buildOptic`, in `opticG` on the gun), an ADS fov, sight height `h` and mouse sensitivity factor, and a screen-centre reticle canvas `#ret` (`drawRet`); scopes draw a dark mask and hide the gun while aiming.
  - **HUD (Siege):** `hudSiege`: top bar with both teams' operator icons (`opIcon`, dead crossed out, defuser carrier marked), round timer (defuser timer when planted), score; bottom-left operator, health + armor bar, gadget chips; `#banner` (`showBanner`/`bannerAll`, round won/lost per client), `#prompt` plant/disable progress, `#hitm` hitmarker (`showHit`), `#dmgd` damage direction (`dmgDir`), kill feed (`showKill`), Tab scoreboard `#board`, site/defuser/ping/spotted/heartbeat markers in `hudUpdate`, `#flashw` flashbang. Enemy footsteps are panned and louder than teammates' (`updateCamera` entity loop).
  - **Weapon/feel:** per-weapon mags and damage (see `WPN`; Assault uses the AR: 30 rounds, 34 dmg, 3 body shots, head x3), recoil, spread bloom, ADS Q or right mouse, lean Z/X, crouch C, jump F, V auto/semi, H bandage (2, no regeneration), suppression blur, fisheye/grain post pass, REC/compass HUD, minimap M. Rifle: CC0 M4A1 (`m4a1.bin`, `m4a1.jpg`, loaded by `fetch`, needs http). Mock relay for local tests: a 40-line Node `ws` server speaking the frames in `relay/worker.js`; use `?relay=ws://127.0.0.1:8787`.
  - **Not built:** map variety (one generated house), down-but-not-out and revive, rappel and hatches, a pistol slot, defender drones, vehicles, touch controls, late join, host migration, result feedback into jjfront. Not hand-played: balance and bot behaviour were only checked in headless bot matches.
