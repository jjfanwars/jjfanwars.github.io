# Rally (`rally/`): notes for Claude

Read `CLAUDE.md` first (shared rules). Split out of it on 17 Nov 2026; keep this file up to date when you change the game.

- **Rally (`rally/`, formerly Pace Notes, linked from the jjfanwars menu as `#bRally` under "More games", 15 Nov 2026 UI pass):** menu is two columns (`.mleft` play/join/code box, `.mright` garage + stage cards + segmented options that drive hidden selects `sStage/sFog/sRole/sGfx`), turntable car preview `Garage3D` (`js/garage3d.js`, own renderer on `#gCar`, paused while `#menu` is hidden), code box `#cheat` unlocks all cars (`pn-all`). HUD: rev ring canvas `drawRev`, stage progress bar with car dots `buildProg`, live delta vs best from a 50 m time trace (`G.trace`, saved as `tr` in `pn-best-*`), callout card `say()` with arrow glyphs, Esc pause `#pause` (solo stops the clock via `G.paused`), finish card with delta, unlock pill and Next stage. Fun pass: chase cam V (`chaseCam`, `pn-cam`; `G.view.update(..., 'x')` shows both crew), ghost of your best run G (`ghostSetup/ghostStep/ghostRecord`, 0.1 s samples of pos+quat in `pn-ghost-<stage>`, one per stage, saved when faster than the stored ghost), medals (`MEDALS` target average speeds over the nominal `len`, `medalT/medalOf/medalDot`, shown on stage cards and the finish card), style popups for drifts (body slip > 0.2 rad for 1 s+) and big air (0.55 s+) in `styleStep`/`pop` (`#style`), totals on the finish card. Medal speeds are guesses, not playtested. Championship (`CHAMPS` stage lists, `RIVALS` fictional names with a skill factor on the silver time, `Champ.run`, `champStart/champStage/champFinish` replace the finish card with standings, no restarts, best position in `pn-champ-<id>`, screen `#champ`). AI cars (`js/bots.js`, `Bots`): real `Car` physics per bot, driver in `Bots.drive` (lookahead steering on a lane, corner speed from heading change ahead with braking distance, `pace` from the rival skill, steers round logs via cached `l.lmin/lmax`), `Bots.watch` recovers stuck/flipped/wrecked bots, all cars `bump` each other and you (`G.rivals`/`G.meAdapt`); solo option `sBots` (Off/3/5), championship puts 3 of the 8 rivals on track and uses their real (or estimated `Bots.eta`) times; you start at the back; live `#pos`, `botStandings` race table on the finish card. Bots share the player car stats (`CAR` is global). Pace tuned only in headless runs (about 65-80 km/h on forest stages). **16 Nov 2026 pass:** obstacles cut to about 1 per 750 m (`nL`, min 400 m apart, only where visible: not in or within 40 m after a grade 1-3 corner), each with a warning triangle 50 m before, an orange flag and red/white bands on the inner end (end `a` is always the inner one); dust about halved (emission, alpha 0.3, life); tyre marks `World.skidMark` (ring buffer InstancedMesh, `skids(c)` in main from wheel `slip` > 0.42). Handling in `Car.step`: `aMax` from surface grip caps steering lock and the yaw assist (was a flat 24 m/s²), yaw assist gain 10 and lateral bleed relax when holding a throttle drift, countersteer assist from `beta`, in the air the nose follows the flight path, landing damps pitch/roll and emits a `land` event (thud + shake), light wheelspin `spinR` when per-wheel drive passes 45% of grip. Headless bench (bot driver on real physics, Node vm with three-r170.js) gave forest about 87 km/h, snow 77. Same day, second pass: windscreen dirt removed; dash switches are six labelled push buttons (`this.toggles` = {cap, led, face}, hit boxes in `hits`, hover glow via `G.view.hoverSw`, keys 1-6 for the driver) on the dash face above the shortened gear lever; the navigator reads the map as a 2D sheet `#navMap` (the `mapC` canvas moved into the DOM while `look.map`), the 3D board only lifts a little; `MapDraw` adds direction under grade circles, a red obstacle badge, the driver's sight ring, the next note ringed, a NEXT CALLS list (`MapDraw.next`), tunnels dashed, cliff hatching. **Maps:** biome `alpine` (tarmac, `cliffs`, `tunnels`, `rails`, `maxG` grade cap) and stages 15-19 appended at the end of `STAGES` (Eagle Pass, Devil's Balcony, Tunnel Run, Canyon Tunnels, Ledge Road; never reorder, saves and `CHAMPS` use indices), championship `pass`. Anything new in `buildStage` uses its own PRNG (`xr`, `cr`) so older stages generate identically. Cliffs: `CS` side per road point (+1 wall on the right), `cliffAt(s)`, wall up / drop down in the heightfield, wall colliders kind 8, guardrails `rails` (kind 12 colliders, `World.buildRails`). Tunnels `S.tunnels` {s0, s1, cap}: hill raised in the heightfield (`tunAt`, rise starts behind the tube wall), wall colliders, `World.buildTunnels` (tube strip, rock cap, portal faces with the arch cut out, lamps), pace note k 't'. Steep terrain gets a rock look in the terrain shader (`aSteep`, world-space uv). City: building boxes were drawn with width and depth swapped (now `BoxGeometry(b.d, ht, b.w)`), `World.buildStreet` (shopfront plinths, awnings, parapets and roof plant, `S.parked` cars with colliders, `S.lights` traffic lights, street trees kind 3); chase cam slides toward the car in cities and tunnels. **Third pass (16 Nov 2026):** collisions in `Car.collide` grade a hit by `head` (normal speed / point speed): scrapes (head < 0.55) get low restitution and friction and lose yaw, damage scales with head, a wreck needs sev > 15·sqrt(tough) and head > 0.6. Driving model `PHYS.real` (menu `sPhys`, `pn-sPhys`; per car via `car.real`, bots are always arcade and their target speed x0.8 in realistic): `realMu(B, onRoad)` (`B.rGrip`/`rOff` or grip x0.58 / off x0.66), no yaw assist, lateral bleed, countersteer or air/landing help, sharper tyre curve with drop-off, strict friction circle, brake lock-up and wheelspin collapse side grip, damage costs power and pulls the steering, slower shifts and keyboard steering. Stages 20-27 (`gim` text shown in the stage info): Midnight Marathon (`tod: 'night'`, stars, headlights + spots on), Monsoon Pass (`wet`: grip x0.8, `World.buildRain/stepRain`, wet tarmac in the terrain shader, raindrops on the glass `dropM`/`this.wet` cleared by the wipers), Frozen Lakes (`ice` zones), River Run (`fords`), Knife Edge (`ridges` + rails both sides), race stages Twin Pines Raceway / Harbour Grand Prix / Dust Bowl Derby (`race`: `over.hw` wide road, no hairpins, no obstacles, crowds at corners). `cfg.over` overrides biome fields per stage. Zones `S.zones` {k, s0, s1, c, y} picked with `xr` after tunnels; `S.zoneAt(s)` gives `mu`/`drag`/`noDrag` used by `Car.step` and `Bots.drive`; lakes flatten the road and a disc of terrain (`World.buildZones`: ice disc, ford water plane), fords dip the road 0.6 m and carve a river, spray in main; notes k 'z'. `S.inTun` covers tunnels and zones, `S.inTube` tunnels only. Slipstream: `Car.bump` sets `this.draft` (3-22 m behind a car, up to -45% drag), HUD `#draft`. Stage cards list 15+ first; default stage 15. Terrain only computed within ~160 m of the road (`far` set) and `near` bails out via `dil`, so long stages build in 3-6 s. **Fourth pass (16 Nov 2026):** driving modes `PHYS.real`/`PHYS.sport` (menu Arcade/Sport/Realistic, Sport default; `pn-sport1` migrates saved prefs once): Sport = `realMu`x1.4, no yaw assist or lateral bleed, tyre curve C 1.3, rear side grip x0.9 / front x1.04, +0.15 rear torque split, wheelspin above 80% grip costs side grip, ABS-style friction circle, anti-spin torque only past ~29 degrees of slide, mild countersteer help; bots stay arcade (`car.sport = false`) at 0.92 pace. Stages 28-34 (append only): Pea Souper (`fogBank`: `S.banks`, `S.fogAt`, `World.atmo` scales fog density), First Light (`sunrise`: `World.atmo` blends TOD night -> dawn -> morning by progress, stars fade), Gorge Bridges (`bridges`), Downhill Dash (`downhill` m: `TD` tilts `baseH` start->finish; `fade`: brake heat `bT`/`fadeK`, HUD `#btemp`), Leap of Faith (`gaps`: kicker profile, gorge with no deck, bots floor it, `S.safeS` puts players back before a gap and bots past it), Zig Zag Wall (`gw` grade weights, hairpins always answered), Rail Crossings (`railx`: zones k 'rail' with `th/per/off/V/len`, `S.trainU(z, t)` nose position, `S.dynNear` moving colliders on `S.clock` = `G.t`, `Car.collide` judges hits on relative speed; `World.buildRail/stepTrains`: ballast, sleepers, rails, barriers and flashing lights, train mesh; bots wait). Terrain has a drawn grid `HV` (gorges) and a driveable grid `H` (bridge decks stay at road height within HW+3); `S.vAt`, `S.groundAt`; `World.buildSpan` (bridge deck in `terrMat` so it matches the road, slab, piers, river; gap lip boards and flags) and `World.buildBackdrop` (12 m coarse ground out to 420 m beyond the grid, same material, so no holes into the sky). Big features need room: `crowded(s0, s1, R)` rejects tunnels/zones near other parts of the road, and gorges/track beds only shape ground whose nearest road is their own stretch.
