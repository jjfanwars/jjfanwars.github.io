- **Ridge Runner (`rally/climb.html`, 16 Nov 2026):** side-view hill-climb minigame (original, in the 2D hill-climb genre: don't copy any specific game's name, art or levels), linked from the Rally menu (`#bClimb`) and the jjfanwars menu (`#bClimb`). One file, canvas 2D, no libraries. `<script id="sim">` is pure (Node-testable: `module.exports` when `module` exists): `STAGES` (g, mu, amp/freq/grow/climb hills, `ice` patches mu x0.42, `craters`, `ridge`, moon `fuel` 0.55), `VEH` (chassis m/L/Hh, wheels {x, y, r, m, dr}, rest/travel/k/c, T/P/vmax, mu, `air` tilt accel, tank/fuelK, head + hr, `hull` points, kart `boost`), `UPS` + `upCost`, `makeSpec(vi, up)`, `Terrain` (0.25 m lazy grid, `at`, `slope`, `closest` signed distance, wall left of x=0), `Rig` (chassis + free wheels on strut springs and stiff lateral springs, motor/brake torque reacts on the chassis, penalty ground contact, friction impulses capped by grip, hull penalty contacts, crash when the head touches or a roof point touches upside down), `Run` (DT 1/360 substeps, fuel, coin clusters 5/25/100, fuel cans spaced 175-430 m, milestones every 100 m, flips/air-time bonuses, events `ev`, end reasons). `<script id="app">`: save `rr-save1` (coins, own, up, st, best, s, v), `Snd` synth, keys/touch pedals/gamepad, `startRun`/`showOver` (next unlock goal bar), particles, camera, canvas drawing (`drawBack/drawGround/drawDecor/drawItems/drawCar` per vehicle style), menu cards with canvas thumbs. `?test` exposes `window.__RR`. Code box `#code` next to DRIVE: the unlock code (same pattern as the Rally one) owns every stage and vehicle and maxes every upgrade; never put the code in What's new. Headless test: extract the sim script and drive `Run.update(1/60, gas, brk, boost)` with a simple tilt-keeping driver. **Update (16 Nov 2026):** stages 5-7 (append only) Mud Bog (`mud` patches: `T.mud`, mu x0.6, `dragAt` 3.2 slows the wheels), Night Woods (`night`: `drawNight` darkens via the offscreen `NC` canvas with a destination-out headlight cone and car glow, items redrawn on top, fireflies), Canyon Jumps (`jumps`: `JUMP0` 78 + `JUMP_P` 150, kicker, pit, climb-out slope, landing slope; `jumpGap/jumpLip`; warning signs). Vehicles 5-6 Dirt Bike (rear drive only, `dr` 0 on the front, exposed head) and Old Bus (`zoom` 0.62, passengers). `Run.stats` (flips, maxAir, maxWheelie, cans, perfect, trickC, mileC), wheelie bonus (front wheel up 1.5 s+), perfect landing (air 0.9 s+, both wheels down within 0.14 rad of the slope), generic stuck (8 s under 0.4 m/s). App: missions `MT` kinds (dist, flips, air, coins, wheelie, cans, perfect, total) in `SV.mis` (3 active, `misN` tier, `checkMissions` every 0.25 s, rewards paid in `showOver`, claimed ones replaced by `fillMissions`), daily bonus (`SV.day/streak`, 150 x streak up to 7), dials `drawGauges` (wide screens), pedals shown on every device (lit from `readInput`), head bobble `R.hb` -> `HB`, crash slow motion 0.35x, pseudo-gear engine voice `gearK`, shadow, grass tufts, coin breakdown `#oC`, stat bars `statBars`, mute `SV.snd`. **Crash leniency + vehicles 7-10 (16 Nov 2026):** the head is a penalty contact (bounces); crash only when `c < 0` (upside down) with the head down 0.25 s (`headT`) or roof points down with `c < -0.5` for 0.8 s (`roofT`); wheelie bar in `Rig.step` (a wheel on the ground and more than 0.85 rad from the slope: restoring torque `ex * I * 30` plus damping); lying on its side or roof (`lying`), gas/brake tilt torque x1.1 rocks it back. Vehicles (append only): Quad Bike, Sports Car, Moon Rover (3 wheels), Tank (5 road wheels, tracks drawn as a band at the mean suspension height). The physics handles any wheel count. The user asked for no power-ups.
