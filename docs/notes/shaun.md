# Last Stand (`shaun/`): notes for Claude

Read `CLAUDE.md` first (shared rules). Keep this file up to date when you change the game.

**Queued work:** before working on this game, check open GitHub issues labelled `shaun-request` (`gh issue list --label shaun-request`). They come from the in-game request box. Close an issue once its request is built.

## What it is (19 Nov 2026)
Live at /shaun. First-person "final stand" horde shooter: an armoured trooper on a sandbag barricade on a desert planet at dusk, holding off endless waves of warrior bugs with a six-barrel rotary cannon. No levels, no upgrades. The player never moves.

The owner's design brief is **brain-off**: no skill needed beyond sweeping the gun left and right.
- **No aim assist (19 Nov 2026):** you aim yaw and pitch yourself (mouse, WASD/arrows, touch drag; `P.pitch` clamp -0.55..0.4). Every round from a person is a ray from the eye (`SLOTX`, 1.7, 0) along yaw/pitch with a little spread (more with heat, less in ADS) tested against three hit spheres per bug (thorax, head, tail; `rayBugs`); it damages the first bug and 60% of the time the next one on the same line. Misses kick dust where the ray meets the ground. Guests send `p` in `aim`; snapshot gunner rows carry pitch x100 as the 5th field. `pickTarget`/auto-pitch now only drive sentry pods (`fireShotAuto`). `g.tgt` is the last bug hit (HUD brackets). Normal bugs have 2 HP, runners 1, brutes 20.
- **Aim down sights (hold Q, touch `#bAimT`):** `P.ads` 0..1 over 0.18 s (`aiming()`), FOV 72 -> 40, gun tucks down, sweep speed x0.5 (`adsSens`), less shake, lock cone 0.045 -> 0.02 rad (`coneOf(g)`; guests send `a` in `aim` so the host uses their cone). Particle point scale follows `cam.fov` every frame.
- **Friendly fire** (`SET.ff`, menu `#ff` / pause `#ff2`, host's setting wins: `G.ff = SET.ff && crewN() > 1`, sent as `ff` in snapshots; guests' checkboxes are disabled). `mateOnLine` finds a squadmate within 0.55 m of the aim line nearer than the target (only at hard side sweeps, the yaw clamp is ±1.5); `friendlyHit` sparks + `hurt(1.2)` on the shared armour per round, event `['f', fromSlot, toSlot]` -> `ffNote` toasts both players (2 s throttle per victim).
- Overheat is cosmetic: at full heat there's a 0.6 s vent (`P.vent`) with steam, then heat drops to 0.55. R or RMB vents by hand.
- **No waves (endless horde, 19 Nov 2026):** spawning never stops. `G.wave` is now a hidden threat level (HUD shows THREAT n) that rises every `LEVEL_T` 28 s (`G.gap` is its timer); `spawnRate(n) = min(55, 15 + 3n) x crewMul`, doubled while fewer than 60 are alive. Level 1 opens with 70 bugs already charging at 60-120 m. No WAVE/HELD banners (`waveCleared` is unused). Titan still every 5th level. At most 720 live bugs; pool `MAXB` = 1100 including corpses. Healing: each squad kill +0.02 armour (host). Kill streak (`myKill`: kills within 1.6 s chain, `G.combo/comboT/comboBest`, HUD number left of the crosshair; every 50 = chime, +4 armour, full shield). Best score is kills now (`ls-bestk`); end card shows best streak, kills, time held.
- Rounds punch through: every round also hits the nearest bug within 2.6 m (x size) of the target (`nearBug`), 40% a third one (`hitFx(..., minor)` = fewer particles).
- The fail state is forgiving. Attackers' summed dps is soft-capped (`4 * (dps/4)^0.5`; warriors 2.2 dps, runners 1.6), drains a shared shield (100, +45/s after 1.2 s without a hit), then hull (`G.hp`) at 0.2x. Under 30 hull: "ARMOUR CRITICAL" banner + alarm (`G.lowWarn`). Hull 0 = OVERRUN end card (wave, kills, time held `G.runT`, best; Space restarts).
- First brute of a run gets a "BRUTE" banner (`bruteWarn`).
- Bot play-test (always sweeping to the nearest bug), after milestone 2: solo lasts about 4 min and dies on wave 11 with ~2000 kills. Call-ins arrive about every 35 s, and armour holds at 100 until wave 9, then slides. The bot ignores the titan unless it is the nearest bug (a human sweeps onto the big bar), so the wave-10 titan usually finishes it. Expect a human to reach wave 6 to 10.

## Milestone 2 content (all brain-off: nothing to press, just sweep)
- **New bugs** (`TYPES` 3-5; type is 3 bits now):
  - Spitter (3, wave 4+): squats at 42 m (`st` 1) and lobs acid globs (`spit`, `GLOBS`, `stepGlobs`: 1.7 s arc, `hurt(7)` on landing, green splash and decal).
  - Burrower (4, wave 6+): spawns underground (`st` 3, body hidden, dust trail, skipped by `pickTarget`/`nearBug`/blasts), erupts at 28 m (`surfaceFx`; guests detect the 3 to 0 change in `applySnap`).
  - The first spitter and the first burrower each get a banner (`newTypeWarn`, event `['n', type]`; `G.seen` bitmask). Spawn odds are in `spawnOne` (p2 brute, p3 spitter, p4 burrower, then runners).
- **Hive titan** (5): spawns at the start of every 5th wave (`spawnTitan`, HP `300 x crewMul x (1 + .25(n/5 - 1))`), 4.3x scale, footstep shake and roars (`stepTitanFx`), HP bar at the top of the HUD (`G.titan`). Aim assist favours it inside 90 m. Gun runs only do 30% damage to it. Its death (`titanDown`, run from `killBug` on host and guests) is the finale: 1.8 s slow-mo (`G.slow` scales dt at the top of `step`), fireball, sky flash, triple boom, a dust shockwave (`G.shock`, `stepShock`: 70 m/s out to 75 m, kills every bug it passes, host side), "TITAN DOWN" banner and full shield + armour.
- **Support call-ins** (`callIn`): squad kills fill `G.sup` (cost `(140 + 20 x wave) x crewMul` kills; sentry kills don't count). When full, the next call-in fires on its own, alternating:
  - Gun run (`gunRun`, `stepRun`, mesh `SHIP`): a dropship strafes along x at the densest z (`pickRunZ`) at 95 m/s, 18 impacts/s, `blast(x, z, 6, 8)` per impact. Kills toss corpses (`fxKill`). Event `['R', z*10, dir]`.
  - Sentry pod (`dropSentry`, `stepSentries`, `makeSentry`): falls onto slot 4 or 5 (`SLOTX` -9.6/9.6) and fires 16 rounds/s at the closest bug for 18 s. It is a gunner in `G.gunners` with id `S4`/`S5` and `g.sentry` set; it is left out of `crewMsg`, `crewN`/`crewMul`, the squad list, successors and migration pruning. Event `['S', slot]`; guests take its yaw/fire from snapshots.
- **Supply canisters** (`CANS`, `dropCan`, `stepCans`, `popCan`): from wave 2 the host drops one every 30-46 s (`G.dropT`), parachuting from 55 m to 16-32 m out. They pop when any squad gunner's spinning gun points within ~0.06 rad + size of one (host decides). They expire after 26 s. Kinds `CANK`: 0 OVERDRIVE (12 s, 60 rounds/s, heat bleeds off, more tracers), 1 SHOCK ROUNDS (12 s, every other hit arcs to 2 more bugs: `shockArc`), 2 ARMOUR CELL (+25 armour, full shield; more likely below 55 armour). `G.buff` = {k, t}. HUD shows a diamond marker and label on screen, plus the buff timer. Events `['D', id, x*10, z*10, k]`, `['O', id]` (popped), `['X', id]` (expired).
- `resetSpecials` (called from `resetWorld`) clears all of it.

## Feel pass (19 Nov 2026, playtester asked for gun weight, bug movement, deaths, atmosphere)
- **Gun weight:** spin-up 0.6 s, run-down 2.2 s. Per round: `thump` (low body, every 2nd), faint dune slapback (every 4th), stronger kick/climb springs, and real recoil: `P.pitch` climbs 0.0008 rad per round (less in ADS) with small yaw wander, so you hold it down. `Sfx.roar` (bandpassed noise loop) under sustained fire; letting go after > 0.35 s plays the `tail` echo (`P.burst`). Muzzle blast lifts dust off the sandbags; gun vibrates while firing; shake grows over 3 s of fire. Brute kills within 70 m give a 60 ms hit-stop (`G.hs`, dt x0.2).
- **Bug movement:** planted-foot gait in `drawBug` (60% stance sliding back at body speed, 40% lifted swing; `b.ph` rate 2.7 x sp / s, stride 0.7), body pitches with the slope and corpse pile, speed surges per bug, and near the line jammed bugs climb over each other (`b.clm` from separation overlap within 14 m of the goal, added to `b.y` on the host so guests get it via snapshots).
- **Deaths:** harder knock-back, half of non-gibbed bugs thrash for 1.2-2.6 s (`b.thr`, big slow leg flails), ichor pumps out for 1.6 s and sliding corpses kick dust (within 70 m), death sounds more often.
- **Atmosphere:** radio chatter (`RADIO` lines, `radio(kind)`: start, idle every 16-30 s, low armour, titan, streak 150s; `radio` buffer is a click + garbled band-limited talk; typed out in a box at the bottom, `RAD`). Dropship flyovers every 35-70 s (`FLY` = clone of `SHIP`, `flyby` sound, drops flares). Other outposts fighting on the horizon (`FLANK`: tracer bursts, flashes, faint crackle). Wind gusts (`gust` drives wind loop gain/cutoff and sheets of blowing dust).

## Visual pass (19 Nov 2026)
- **Baked terrain lighting:** `bakeAt` marches a soft sun shadow across the heightfield (sun is ~6 degrees up, so dunes throw long shadows) and computes 8-direction horizon AO, stored per vertex in a `bake` attribute (x = sun visibility, y = AO) on both terrain meshes. `bakedShader` (groundM `onBeforeCompile`) multiplies direct light by x and indirect by y, adds a macro anti-tiling sample, `../tex/ground_c.jpg` detail and darker red steep faces. `BK` grid + `sunVis(x, z)` lets bugs (instance colour) and rocks darken in dune shadow.
- **Mesas:** 40x22 segment eroded cylinders with gullies, base terraces, baked strata colours and base AO (vertex colours), `wall_n` normal map; 7 nearer buttes at 480-700 m; a far ragged ridge ring at ~3.3 km lost in haze. Fog is lighter (`FogExp2` 0.00105, warmer colour) so the strata read.
- **Sky:** streaky high cloud/dust layers (`fb` noise, lit hot near the sun, drift with `ct`), a pale banded gas giant low in the east.
- **Post:** light shafts in `compM` (32-tap radial blur of pixels brighter than 1.1 toward the sun's screen position, `sun`/`sunI` set in `render`).
- **Bugs:** soft contact shadows (`SHD` instanced pool, one per drawn bug, shrinks while leaping). Rocks: 420, vertex AO + `prop_n`. First-person gun is rougher/less metallic so it doesn't glare into the sun.

## Textures (19 Nov 2026)
- All procedural at load, tileable: `genTex(N, f, normalStrength)` takes a height + colour + roughness function and returns `{map, nrm, rgh}` (normal map from height differences). Helpers `pn`/`pf` (periodic value noise/fbm), `vor` (periodic voronoi).
- `SAND` (1024 on High, 512 Low): wind ripples, coarse grains in troughs, pebbles; used by the terrain with its own normal + roughness maps (ground_c.jpg stays as fine detail). `BURL`: woven jute for sandbags. `CRATE`: chipped olive paint. `STEEL`: scraped plates with rust.
- `triMat(material, tex, scale, bump, key, toneAmt, fade)`: object-space triplanar detail (R height -> derivative bump, G roughness, B tone), works on instanced meshes. Bump fades out past `fade` metres to avoid sparkle. Used with `CHIT` (armour plates/pits/scratches) on bug bodies and `ROCK` (cracks/layers) on rocks and mesas. Legs use plain `legMat` (stretched cylinders).
- Fix: the frame loop clamps dt at 0 (a slow load made the first rAF timestamp earlier than `last`, giving a negative dt that blew up shake and time).

## Detail pass (19 Nov 2026)
- Near bug body (`bugBodyGeo(false)`) adds overlapping thorax armour plates with bone ridges, side plates + spiracles, lateral spikes and spiracles on the tail segments, belly plates, head crest, eye clusters, jaw serrations, palps and neck tendons. The far LOD is unchanged.
- Near bugs get detailed legs: `legGeoHi` (lathe profile with muscle bulge, knee knob, joint collar, tapering spike, barbs), pools `LUPH`/`LLOH` (NEARB x 6), counted with `DRAW.lh`; far bugs keep the plain cylinders.
- Gun (`makeGun`): bevelled parts (`RB` = extruded rounded box), rail with slots, side plates and bolts, ejection port, finned motor housing with bolt circle and power cable, open vented heat shroud, barrels with muzzle sleeves and bores, flash cage, holo sight with red dot, ribbed front grip, trigger + guard, ribbed ammo drum with latch and handle, segmented feed chute, round brass belt, gloved hands with fingers on both grips, arm cuffs.
- Gotcha: a `//` comment inside a one-line GLSL replacement in `triMat` commented out the closing brace, so the shader failed and bug bodies, rocks and mesas vanished (fixed). Check the console for `Shader Error` after shader edits.

## Survival mode (19 Nov 2026, solo only)
- Menu button `#bSurv` sets `G.mode = 'surv'` (`SURV()`); `bStart` and any squad set `'horde'`. Best = highest wave (`ls-bestS`, `G.bestS`). Death = `overrun` -> KILLED IN ACTION card.
- Player: `P.foot` (on foot) vs on the cannon. `toggleMount` (E, touch USE `#bUseT`) within 2.3 m of the cannon. On foot: `survMove` (WASD, Shift sprint, touch left-side stick `mId` -> `P.mvX/mvY`), free 360 yaw and +-1.35 pitch, `P.eyeY` from terrain + bob, can't cross the sandbag line (`inLine`, go round the ends), bugs shove you, 160 m leash.
- Rifle (`RIF` = `makeRifle`, first person; `MOUNT` = cannon left standing on a tripod): `rifleStep` 10 rounds/s, mag 30 (`SV.mag`), reserve `SV.res`, R reload 1.8 s, single-target rays from the eye (`fireShot` uses P.x/eyeY/P.z when `foot`), spread grows with sustained fire and moving, real climb. Cannon has a finite belt `SV.belt` (1200 start).
- Waves: `survWave` (quota 26 + 16n, spawned over 22 + 2n s, packs from all directions with a front bias, titan every 5th), `survSpawn`: when 2 or fewer remain (or 70 s after the quota is out) -> 25 s lull (`SV.lull`), shield refilled, crates scattered. Bugs target the player (`P.x/P.z`), attack within 0.9 + 0.55 x size, crawl slowly over the sandbags; spitters lob at the player from 30 m (globs only hurt within 2.6 m). Hull takes 0.45x after the shield (0.2x in horde), shield regen 18/s after 3 s.
- Crates (`SV.items`, `addItem`, `scatterItems`, `ITEMK`): rifle ammo +90, cannon belt +500, med kit +35, armour cell (full shield +10); walk over them on foot. 7 in the compound + 4 out in the field at start, 5 field + 2 compound each lull. HUD diamonds with distance within 90 m, ammo readout, wave/contacts or scavenge timer, `[E] MAN THE CANNON` prompt.

## Multi-night survival (19 Nov 2026)
- A night = `WPN` (3) waves with 12 s breathers (`SV.nw`, `SV.night`). After the 3rd: `startDay` (dawn callout, +20 health, full shield, 8 field + 3 compound crates) and a `DAY_T` 90 s day (`SV.day`): no spawns, bugs flee (AI target flips away, freed past 170 m), `[N]` skips. `nightFall` -> next night, waves keep the global `G.wave` count for difficulty.
- Perks at dawn (`PERKS`, `SV.offer` 3 random unowned, keys 1-3 or the `#perks` buttons, `pickPerk`, `has(id)`): extended mags (45), fast hands (1.1 s reload), field medic (bigger med kits + regen), heavy belt, armour plating (-25% damage), marathon, AP rounds (rifle pierce), scavenger (+3 crates/day), ammo cache.
- Lighting (`survLight`, every frame): `dayK` eases to 0 at night / 1 by day; scales `compM.exp`, `SUN` and `HEMI` (sun direction stays, so baked shadows still fit). Night flashlight `FLASH` (SpotLight on the camera, origin pushed 1.3 m forward so it doesn't blow out the gun; `T` toggles `P.torch`). Horde mode stays at 0.5 (unchanged look).
- Score = nights survived (`ls-bestN`).

## Code map (`shaun/game.js`; UI in `shaun/index.html`)
- **Renderer:** own post chain (`render`). The scene goes to a HalfFloat MSAA RT, then a bright pass, two blur levels, and the composite `compM`, which does ACES, gamma, vignette, grain, chromatic aberration and the damage tint. The renderer itself has `NoToneMapping`. Low quality (`SET.gfx`) lowers the pixel ratio and turns off shadows and MSAA.
- **World:**
  - Sky is a ShaderMaterial (`skyM`, sun disc, glow, `flash` uniform for artillery). It also feeds a PMREM environment map.
  - Terrain height grid `HG`/`gH` (2.5 m, ±300 m) comes from `H0`: flat near the base, dunes beyond. There is a far plane underneath.
  - Also: mesas, instanced rocks, sandbag arc (instanced), concrete and plates using `../tex/prop_*`, a crashed dropship with `FIRES`, burning hulks, two searchlight beams (`BEAMS`), flares (`flareL`) and horizon artillery flashes.
- **Bugs:** compact instanced pools, refilled every frame between `drawBegin`/`drawEnd` (which set `.count`, so unused slots cost nothing; `b.idx` is only an id slot now). `BODY` = detailed body (`bugBodyGeo(false)`, first `NEARB` 260 bugs within `NEAR_D` 48 m, casts shadows), `BODYL` = low-poly body for the rest, `LUP`/`LLO` (upper and lower leg segments, placed with `segMat`).
  - Corpse physics (`stepBugsCommon`): corpses live `CORPSE_T` 14 s. Settled corpses build `PILE` (1.4 m height grid, `pileAt`); live bugs climb it (`b.py`, smoothed; `drawBug`/`bugCenter` add it for the living, corpses bake it into `b.y` when they die) and falling corpses land on top. `CGRID` lets moving live bugs shove corpses aside (push + spin). Kills add knock-back away from the shooter (`killBug`, scaled by 1/size^2); bounces kick dust (`dustPuff`). `blast` also throws corpses within 1.5 r.
  - `drawBug` handles gait (tripod), claws slashing in the attack state (`st` 1), leaps (`st` 2), dead flip with curling, twitching legs, and sinking after 6 s. `b.legs` is a 6-bit mask, so legs can be blown off.
  - Look (the owner wants Starship Troopers-style warrior bugs, not spiders): `BH` 1.5, slim armoured thorax with spine spikes, long tail that sweeps up behind to a spike, low wedge head with four pincers. Walking legs are spread fore and aft (`LEGS` foot z 1.5 / -1.75) with knees high above the back (`ky = BH + 1.3`) and long spike lower legs; the two front scythe arms are raised and stab down when attacking (`st` 1).
  - `TYPES`: 0 warrior, 1 runner/leaper, 2 brute.
  - Hit reaction: `hitFx` sets `b.hk` (decays in `stepBugsCommon`); `drawBug` snaps the head up, shoves the body back and jitters the roll. Brutes throw armour sparks. Host side, `damageBug` staggers (`b.stag`: 35% speed for 0.35 s, brutes 0.15 s) and shoves light bugs back 4 cm per round, so a hosed bug visibly struggles forward.
  - Limp: missing walking legs make the bug lean toward that side and lurch (speed pulses with the gait).
  - Death: `killBug` gives the corpse momentum (`dvx/dvz/dvy`: slides forward at 70% of its run speed, small hop, leapers fall out of the air, bounce), a tumble target `droll` (on its back or its side) and yaw spin `dspin`. 25% of warriors and 45% of runners gib instead (`b.gib`: body vanishes, every leg and extra chunks fly, big slime burst, freed after 1 s). Brute deaths add dust, a low thump and camera shake when within 60 m.
  - Separation checks the 3x3 neighbour cells (`cell()` uses floor, so no double-size cell at 0).
  - Host AI: `stepBugsHost` (spawning, waves, grid separation, attacking at the barricade, `hurt`).
- **Effects:** `Parts` point systems. `PA` is additive (sparks, fire, embers), `PS` is smoke and dust, and `PG` is slime blobs that stick to the ground. Also goo decals `DEC`, carapace chunks `CHK`, blown-off legs `LGD`, tracers `TRC` and hot casings `CAS` (with tinkle sounds). Hit and death effects are in `hitFx`/`deathFx`.
- **Gun:** `makeGun(fp)` is used for the first-person view (with gauntlets) and for remote marines (`makeMarine`). `updateGunVisual` handles barrel spin, heat glow on `barrelM`, and the muzzle flash sprites. `stepFeel` handles recoil springs (kick, muzzle climb, camera pitch and yaw), trauma shake, sway, breathing, FOV punch, drift and the muzzle light.
- **Audio:** `Sfx` is all procedural WebAudio buffers: shot (3 variants), tink, squelch, crunch, squeal, screech, chitter, boom, zap and horn, plus loops for wind, hiss and rattle and a motor whine from oscillators (`Sfx.gun`). It starts on first input. `SET.vol` is the master volume.
- **HUD:** one 2D canvas (`drawHud`): crosshair bloom, hit and kill markers, damage-direction arcs, a motion tracker with sweep (front arc, 200 m), shield, armour segments, heat, RPM, auto-fire state, wave, kills, squad list and scrolling readouts. The CSS visor edge and scanlines are in index.html.
- **Screens:** menu (start and squad buttons sit at the top so they are visible at 540 px tall), pause (ESC or the touch `II` button `#bPauseT`), end card `showEnd(title, sub, w, k, t, best)` used for OVERRUN and SIGNAL LOST. Callouts (`callout`) shrink for long text.
- **Settings:** stored in localStorage `ls-set` (sens, vol, gfx, auto, name). The best wave is in `ls-best`.

## Multiplayer (squad, up to 4)
- Uses the shared relay `wss://jjfanwars-relay.businessautomationvic.workers.dev` with rooms at `/room/<5 letters>`. The `Net` module is copied from `hollow/game.js` and uses the same frame format. The worker did not change.
- **Host squad:** the game starts at once, and the code and `?room=CODE` link are copied and shown in the pause menu and HUD. Friends join mid-fight.
- **Join:** the player enters the code (or it is prefilled from `?room=`). The host caps the squad at 3 guests.
- **Host-authoritative:**
  - The host runs the bug AI and resolves every gunner's shots. Guests send `{t:'aim', y: yaw, f: fire}` at 15 Hz; the host fires for them with the same `pickTarget`.
  - The host broadcasts `packSnap()` at 12 Hz: flat bug array `[id, x*10, z*10, yaw*100, type | st<<3 | legs<<5 | hp<<11, y*10]`, events `e` (`['k', id, slot]` kills, `['l', id, leg]`, `['w', wave]`, plus the milestone 2 events above), gunners `[id, yaw*100, fire, kills]` (sentries included), shield, hp, kills, wave, and the state a guest needs to take over: `q` quota, `gp` gap, `cl` cleared, `bs` brute seen, `rt` run time, `sp`/`sn` support meter, `bf` buff, `dt` next drop timer.
  - Guests interpolate bug positions (`stepBugsGuest`) and run every gunner's shots as visual-only effects locally (same target pick), so splatter is instant. Only kills and HP come from the host.
- Other messages:
  - `crew` (id, slot, name list) places the marines at `SLOTX`.
  - `start` (sent to a joiner), `over`, `restart` (the host clicks restart).
- Shield and hull are shared by the squad. Kills are shown per player and in total.
- Snapshot events also carry `['c', wave]` (wave held), `['b']` (first brute) and `['a']` (armour critical) so guests get the same banners.
- **Host migration** (milestone 2; the relay worker is unchanged). It works because a new `?role=host` socket on the same room code makes the relay send 'host up' to every guest, and they re-send `hello`.
  - Join order: the host gives each new peer `j` (`Net.seq`); `crew` rows are `[id, slot, name, j]`. Successor = the guest with the lowest `j` (`successors()`).
  - A host that quits (`bQuit`, `pagehide`) sends `{t:'bye', next}` first, so the successor takes over at once (`becomeHost`). Others wait up to 6 s for the new host's 'host up'.
  - A host that just drops: guests get `_hostgone`; the successor promotes after 1.5 s, the next in line 2.5 s after that, and so on (`'host up'` cancels via `_hostback`).
  - `becomeHost`: `Net.promote` closes the guest socket and opens a host socket on the same code. The local gunner is re-keyed to `'H'` (keeps slot and kills), the old `'H'` marine is removed, bug positions settle to the last snapshot, `nextId`/`canId` carry on, and the wave state comes from the last snapshot. Bug HP comes from snapshots too (`hp<<11`). `G.migT` 6 s later drops gunners who never said hello again.
  - Returning members: `hello` carries `slot`, `kills` and `j` (`Net.helloX`), so they keep their place and kills. The old host, if it comes back: one quick retry as host, then `_hostlost` makes it rejoin as a guest (`rejoinAsGuest`; it re-hosts if the room has no host, error 4404). If it is replaced anyway (close code 4001), `_replaced` makes it a guest.
  - SIGNAL LOST is no longer shown on host loss (a guest always takes over). `G.lost` and the card's Back to menu path still exist.
  - The guest ignores the relay's 'host up' that arrives right after connecting (it used to send `hello` twice, so the host sent `start` twice).

## Testing
- `?test` exposes `window.__LS` = {start, fire(bool|null), step(dt, n, noRender), setYaw, host, join(code), G, P, bugs, Net, Sfx, spawnBug, killBug, startWave, callIn, spawnTitan, dropCan, CANS, gunRun, dropSentry}. Screenshots need one rendered `step(1/60, 1, false)` first (noRender steps leave the canvas black).
- Migration test: 3 browser contexts host/join through the real relay; quit the host (`#bQuit`) or kill its socket (`Net.role = 'solo'; ws.onclose = null; ws.close()`), keep calling `step` on the rest and check `Net.role`, the crew and wave state. A client `ws.close()` against the relay may not fire `close` quickly, so call `ws.onclose({code: 1006})` by hand to simulate a drop.
- Port 8765 may already be taken by another clone's server; use your own port. `step(1/20, 100, true)` simulates 5 s without rendering (fast); a bot that turns `P.yaw` toward the nearest bug each step is a good stand-in for a brain-off player.
- Two pages in one headless browser can host and join through the real relay (the host page's clipboard write is refused headless; that's caught).
- To drive frames by hand, stub `requestAnimationFrame` and then call `step`.
- Headless Chromium needs `--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`. It is very slow at this scene size, so keep the step counts small.
