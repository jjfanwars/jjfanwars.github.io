## Code map (space/game.js)
- **Frames:** inertial world with the planet centre at 0 and a fixed sun direction `SUN`. Bodies (`PLANET`, `MOON` in `BODIES`) have `c` (centre) and `q` (rotation) from `bodyState(b, t)`. The planet spins about +Y (`PLANET.day` = 3600 s). The moon is on rails (`MOON.orbit` 400 km), tidally locked.
- **Floating origin:** the camera is always at 0. `G.O` = camera inertial position; each body group sits at `b.c - G.O`. Never put big coordinates in vertex data.
- **Scale (owner picked 10x, 20 Nov 2026):** planet R 600 km, g 9.81, orbit speed ~2.4 km/s (~10 min to orbit), day 6 h (`day` 21600), air top `atmo` 22 km (HR 2500, HM 500, Rayleigh x3.2 Earth, ozone layer `BO` centred 7.8 km). Moon R 200 km, g 1.63, orbit 12,000 km (~38 h period). `FAR` 6e7. Terrain `maxL` 15 (planet) / 14 (moon). Clouds at 2.2 km.
- **Terrain:** `heightAt(b, x, y, z)` (unit direction, body-fixed) and `colorAt` (also fills `_wt`, material weights grass/sand/rock/snow). Cube-sphere quadtree (`Node`, `updateTree`, `updateTerrain(budgetMs)`), 32x32 chunks with skirts, baked AO from neighbour heights, sea mesh per chunk. `terrMat` shader blends 4 textures (colour as a detail ratio on top of the vertex colour, plus normal maps on High). UVs are chunk-local, snapped to 512 m so they stay small.
- **Space centre:** `SITE` direction (equator), `buildSite()`. Site frame: x east, y up, z south; `siteToBody`/`bodyToSite`. Terrain under it is a true flat plane (`SITE_H + d²/2R`). Collision boxes in `site.boxes`; launch pads in `site.pads`. Helpers: `box`, `cyl`, `mergeGeos`/`beamGeo`/`boxGeo` (one draw call for lattices), `signTex`/`logo`/`decal` (canvas signs).
- **Visual pass (20 Nov 2026, owner wants realistic and stunning above all):**
  - `bakeSky()`: Milky Way (band, bulge, dust lanes, red/blue nebulae) + 3 star layers baked into a HalfFloat cube map, one face per frame (`G.skyBake`), used as `scene.background`. Fades in daylight via `scene.backgroundIntensity`.
  - Shared GLSL: `GLSL_ATMO` (`rs`, `scatter`) and `GLSL_NOISE` (`h31`, `vn`, `fbm3`); `atmoU()` gives the uniforms. `SUN_I` = sun irradiance in the same units as the directional light (3.4); keep them matched or the sky washes out the ground.
  - Sun: limb-darkened disc + glow in `atmoM`; glare, starburst and 6 lens ghosts in `compM` (visibility from depth taps around the sun).
  - Eye adaptation: `lumM` -> `avgM` (log average, centre weighted, eased) in `POST.ad0/ad1`; exposure = .16 / adapted, clamped .02..3.
  - Bloom at 1/4 and 1/16 res. SSAO (`ssaoM`, half res, 12 taps, High only) multiplies the scene before the atmosphere pass.
  - `updateEnv`: PMREM sky reflections from the real scattering (`envMat`), every 1.5 s.
  - Clouds: `makeClouds` shell at `CLOUD_H` 2.6 km, child of the planet group. Two passes: depth only where thick (`DEPTHONLY`, c > .6), then colour with no depth writes. Per-pixel sunlight through the air (sunset colours) and planet shadow.
  - Ocean: `waveTex` (tileable wave normals made at load), 3 scrolling samples in `waterMat`, UVs per chunk (/64 m).
  - Moon: `CRATERS` (700, power-law sizes, `young` ones with ray streaks), `mareAt` (dark basalt plains), `microCraters` (3 scales, 2x2x2 cells). `CR_NEAR` = craters near the chunk being built (speed). `moonMat` = grey-only texture detail (`customProgramCacheKey` 'moon').
  - Test hooks: `YE.prebuild(ms)` builds terrain synchronously; `G.noGrav` freezes the player.
- **Photo-matching pass (20 Nov 2026):** compared with NASA photos (ISS limb, sun glint, Apollo surface, Earth from lunar orbit). Thin atmosphere (`atmo` 7.5 km, HR 950, HM 190, Earth-like column density) so the limb is a crisp band; clouds at 1.5 km; lower relief (mountains ~2 km, snow 1.35 km+). Metering floor .012 so a lit planet isn't blown out next to black space; stars dimmed so they only show when you look away from bright things. Cloud shadows on ground + sea (`cloudShadow`, `CLOUDU`, `bd` attribute = body direction), broader far glint. Moon warmer and with a 4th micro-crater scale; no fill light in space. Shadow map +-260 m, texel snapped.
  - Ocean too light (owner): the cause was exposure, not the water. Auto exposure re-normalises everything, so tweaks to water/air looked identical; exposure key is now .1 (was .16), which keeps clouds unclipped and the sea dark. Water also has a dark deep sea floor, opacity .97, and its sky reflection scaled x.12 in the shader (`radiance`/`iblIrradiance`; three ignores `envMapIntensity` with `scene.environment`). Check exposure first when something looks too bright or dark.
  - Sun matched to ISS photos: small blown-out white disc, tight halo, two faint thin spike pairs (no big starburst).
  - Sunset: ozone absorption (pink/violet twilight); clouds get a pink-violet twilight term; ground exposure capped at dusk (`EMAX` 3.5 in the air, 14 in space).
  - Per-pixel sunlight on terrain + sea (`sunTrans` in the `cloudShadow` injection): reddened through the air and cut at the terminator; the lights loop multiplies `directLight.color` by `gSunT` = sunTrans / camera transmittance (`CLOUDU.camT`).
- **Nebula + light rays (owner: "Interstellar type", 20 Nov 2026):** emission nebula in `bakeSky` (centred on inertial dir (-.42,.5,.76), tilted ellipse, nested domain warping, pink H-alpha / teal OIII / gold, bright rims, dust, faint cluster). Light shafts: `raysM` at 1/4 res (56-step radial march from each pixel toward the sun over sky pixels, jittered then blurred twice), added in `compM` (`rOn`, `tR`); strength 1.8 in air, .4 in space; High only. Sun bloom capped (bright pass clamps at 6) so the sun can't wash the screen white.
- **Volumetric clouds (High, 20 Nov 2026; owner wants Blue Marble style, not "lil dots"):** `WX` = planet weather map (2048x1024 equirect) baked once on the GPU by `weatherM`: domain-warped fbm stretched east-west, 20 cyclone spirals in the storm tracks (±30-60°), cloudy equator, clearer subtropics. `CLOUD3D` = 64³ tileable Perlin-Worley + Worley texture made at load. `GLSL_CLOUD` (`clWeather`, `clDens`) is shared by `cloudM` (half-res ray march, layer `CL.base`-`CL.top` 1.5-4.3 km, 64 steps, light march to the sun, multiple-scattering + powder, sky ambient, sun reddened through the air) and the terrain cloud shadows (`cshadow`). `cloudBlurM` softens it; `atmoM` splits the air at the cloud layer (clouds get aerial perspective); `compM` hides sun glare behind clouds. `CL.cov` = cover. The old shell clouds (`makeClouds`) are Low only. God-ray threshold raised so bright clouds don't veil the screen.
- **Anti-aliasing:** FXAA (`fxaaM`) on the final image: compM renders to `POST.ldr`, then FXAA to the screen. Do NOT use MSAA on `POST.main`: the multisampled depth texture did not resolve on the owner's GPU, so the atmosphere pass saw every pixel as sky and fogged the whole screen.
- **Performance (High):** shadow map re-renders every 2nd frame (`G.frameN`; matrices only change with the map, so no swimming), terrain build budget 4 ms (Low 3), PMREM sky every 3 s, moon bake 1.5 ms/frame, cloud light march reuses the sample's cover, adaptive resolution `G.rs` (.65-1, checked every 3 s from `fps`; `setupPost` rebuilds targets). `YE.bench(n)` times `render()`.
- **Nebula toned down (owner hated the bright paint-swirl look):** muted hydrogen red with a little teal, smaller, brightness .017; space exposure cap `EMAX` 11.
- **Moon lighting fix:** the Moon shares `terrMat`'s shader; `IS_MOON` skips the planet's air/cloud terms. `sunL.color` is clamped to >= .002 (it is the camera's sun transmittance; at 0 the Moon went black when the camera was on the planet's night side).
- **Far Moon (`MI` in flight.js):** beyond 3000 km a smooth 256x128 sphere replaces the terrain chunks, with colour + slope maps baked from `heightAt`/`colorAt` (1024x512, a few rows per frame via `moonBake`, crater list filtered per row) and Lommel-Seeliger lunar shading.
- **Cabin look pass:** panelled lining (canvas colour + normal map with bevelled seams and fasteners), rounded parts (`rbox`), instanced toggle switches/guards/LEDs on the overhead band (`wallParts`), glossy display glass (`screenGlass`), LED strips, flush cabinets.
- **Shadow square fix:** `THREE.ShaderChunk.shadowmap_pars_fragment` is patched at the top of game.js so shadows fade out toward the shadow-map edge (the 520 m shadow square no longer shows).
- **Render:** log depth buffer, HalfFloat target with depth texture. Post: `atmoM` ray-marched single scattering (Rayleigh + Mie, planet shadow) uses scene depth for aerial perspective; then bloom and ACES (`compM`). View rays use the near plane (far-plane unprojection is NaN with this near/far). `sunColour` = CPU transmittance for the sun light colour. `updateEnv` = PMREM sky reflections every 2 s.
- **Mission control (walkable, 20 Nov 2026):** in `buildSite`, x -97..-67, z 59..81. Door at the south-east (x -73.5..-71), window band facing the pads (east). Ground floor: carpet, wood wainscot, ceiling light panels, 3 tiers of 6 consoles (2 live monitors + name plate + chairs each), big front screens (`map`, `graph`, `tele`, `net`, `orbit`), lobby (reception, model rocket, plants, mission patches). Stairs up the west wall (20 boxes, rail) to the glass viewing deck (see-through panes with collision, wood floor, pendant lamps, observation desk facing the pads, telescope, meeting table, hanging map screen, planet globe painted by `mapBuild` (`MAPB.tex`), sofas, kitchenette). Indoor lighting via `interiorMat` with `INT_MC` (downstairs) / `INT_DECK` (glass deck).
- **Player:** `P` (body-fixed pos/vel, `fwd` tangent kept by parallel transport, pitch), `stepPlayer`, `placeCamera`. Time warp keys are in flight.js.

## Flight (space/flight.js, loaded after game.js; shares its globals and calls `boot()` at the end)
- **Physics:** `R` = the rocket (inertial `pos`/`vel` of the centre of mass, `q`, local angular rate `w`). States `pad`/`landed` (latched to a body: body-fixed `lp`/`lq`, `latch(t)`), `fly`, `dead`. `rocketStep(h, t)`: point gravity from planet + moon (`gravAt`, moon on rails via `moonPos`/`moonVel`), thrust with Isp blended by pressure (`airAt`, scale height 3.5 km, zero above `atmo`), drag on air-relative velocity (`groundVel` = co-rotating air), parachute (`R.chute` 0/1 armed/2 open, opens below ~9 km and under 280 m/s as a drogue (`chA` .07), full canopy below 1 km, lands at 7 m/s). Substeps .02 s, or .5 s when coasting in vacuum above 15 km. Liftoff when thrust/weight > 1.02.
- **Rotation:** arcade rates (W/S pitch, A/D yaw, Q/E roll, max .55 rad/s, 1.4 rad/s² accel). SAS `R.sas`: hold / pro / retro / up / off (keys 1-4, T). Fins weathervane the stack into the airflow; a lone capsule flies blunt end first.
- **Contact:** bottom of the stack vs `groundR`. Over 12 m/s or tipped over: `explode()` (pieces become debris, fireball via `boom`, the wreck point is latched to the ground so the camera turns with the planet, respawn after 4 s at the space centre with a new rocket). Otherwise it sets down upright (`landed`).
- **Stages:** `STAGES` (Booster 330 kN / 15 t fuel, Upper stage 60 kN / 3.8 t, Capsule with chute), `buildStage(i)` makes the meshes (lathe bells with inside glow, canvas-painted tanks, fins, capsule windows, chute). `stage()` = launch / drop / ignite / arm chute; dropped stages fly on as `R.debris` (`debrisStep`). About 5 min to orbit with fuel to spare (Ap/Pe tested by a scripted ascent).
- **Visuals:** additive plume shader (`PLUME_VS/FS`, two layers, widens in vacuum, shock diamonds at sea level; hydrolox upper stage is pale violet), `flameL` point light (always in the scene: adding lights later recompiles all materials). Particles `PT` (900, camera-relative billboards, premultiplied alpha, sorted far to near): exhaust smoke (billows along the pad), fire and explosion smoke (`puff`, `boom`).
- **Camera:** `flightCamera` orbits the rocket (drag to look, wheel/pinch zoom), horizon level via a parallel-transported reference `R.cam.ref`, shake from thrust and max-q, kept above ground.
- **Controls:** walk to the pad (label over the rocket), F gets in/out (out only when landed), Space stage, Shift/C throttle, Z/X full/cut (no Ctrl: Ctrl+W closes the tab), Backspace new rocket when not flying, `,` `.` (or `[` `]`) time warp (`WARPS`; max 4x under thrust, in air or below 12 km; 1000x on foot).
- **HUD:** `#fl` panel (stage name, altitude, speed (surface below 20 km), Ap/Pe from `orbitOf` of the dominant body (moon inside `SOI_M`), throttle + fuel bars, next stage action, SAS buttons), `#mPro`/`#mRet` velocity markers, `#rTag` rocket label. Sound: `sndInit` (WebAudio noise) rumble/crackle/wind loops + `sfx` one-shots.
- **Capsule cabin (owner asked for detailed, walkable interiors, 20 Nov 2026):** capsule is 5 m wide (`CAB`: cone 21.85-25.45, floor 22.0, ceiling 25.15; flared adapter on the upper stage). `shellGeo(inset, keep, uvr)` builds the cone quad by quad so windows (`CWIN`) and the hatch (`CHATCH`, at the back) are real holes/panels; outer shell, frames, glass, padded lining (`CM.lining`, canvas), reveals, floor, ceiling light ring, handrails, overhead switch band (emissive canvas), 3 seats (`SEATS`: Pilot / Navigator / Engineer) with tilted consoles showing live screens, stowage bags, extinguisher. `cabL` = cabin point light (always in the scene). Interior materials use `interiorMat(m, INT_CAB)` (game.js): scales sky/IBL light (no occlusion indoors) and adds a fill colour.
- **Crew:** `C` (in, seat, view, local pos/vel/yaw/pitch). `cabinStep`: walk with the felt acceleration `R.felt` (thrust + drag in rocket-local coords; gravity when latched), weightless float when it is under .6 m/s². Walls = floor, ceiling, cone radius at head height. F (`cabinAct`/`cabinNear`): sit, get up, open hatch (not in flight). Only the Pilot seat flies (`pilot()` gates keys, throttle and staging); V toggles the outside chase view. `cabinCamera` puts the eye in rocket-local coords. `#act` = interaction prompt.
- **Live screens:** `SCR`/`screenMat(name, k)` canvas textures shared by the cabin and mission control, redrawn every .35 s only when someone is near (`updateScreens`): `tele`, `orbit` (orbit drawn from the elements), `sys`, `graph` (`HIST`), `map` (ground track on a planet map baked a row at a time by `mapBuild`, night side shaded), `net`, `code`, `wx`.
- **Test menu (flight.js `TESTS`, `testMenu`):** ` key in game or "Test mode" on the main menu. Jumps: pilot seat on the pad, inside the capsule, 80 km orbit (`flyFrom(1)`), re-entry, parachute landing, Moon orbit, walk on the Moon, mission control, glass deck, space centre; time of day (`setHour`, `hourNow`), cloud cover, refuel; controls list. Handy for headless tests too: `YE.TESTS.orbit()`.
- **Testing:** `YE.board()`, `YE.stage()`, `YE.FLIGHT.step/camera/visuals` to fast-forward synchronously (headless fps is too low for real-time flight).
- **Testing:** `YE.placeAt(x, y, z, yaw, pitch)` in site coords. Headless Chromium is slow with this renderer: take one screenshot at a time with a long screenshot timeout.
- Textures: `space/tex/` (CC0 Poly Haven, see `space/THIRD_PARTY.md`).
