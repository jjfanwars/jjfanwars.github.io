Shaker doors, stone tops and the odd arched bar.

Hamptons Cabinetry is a cabinet maker in Belmont. Most of our work is kitchens, but we also do bathroom vanities, laundries and home bars across Geelong.

We like classic profiles and real materials: painted shaker doors, timber, stone benchtops, reeded glass and brushed brass. If you've got something else in mind, we'll make that too.

How a job runs

  1. 01 Get in touch Email us a few photos and rough measurements, or ask us to come out and measure up.
  2. 02 Drawings & quote We draw the layout, help you pick doors, tops and handles, and give you a written quote.
  3. 03 Build Once you sign off, we make the cabinetry to the exact measurements of your room.
  4. 04 Install We fit it, line it all up and clean up after ourselves.
