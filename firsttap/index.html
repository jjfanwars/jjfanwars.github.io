Questions clinics ask

Does it replace Halaxy or Cliniko?

No. Your practice software stays the home for your diary, notes, invoices and Medicare claims. Firsttap works on top: it reads free times and writes bookings into the same diary.

What stops two people getting the same time?

The first tap wins, checked in one step on our side and again by your practice software before the booking is made. Everyone else gets a kind note that the time's gone and stays on the waitlist.

Will clients get too many texts?

No. Each person has a weekly limit, quiet hours are respected, and after a month on the list they get a "still keen?" text so offers only go to people who still want a spot. Anyone can opt out at any time.

Does AI decide who gets an appointment?

No. Clear rules you can see and change decide who's offered a time: the right practitioner, day, time and session length, longest waiting first. AI is only used in Plus, to draft replies a person checks, with client names removed first.

Where is client data stored?

In Australia. We hold the minimum we need to run refills, such as names, mobile numbers and preferred times, not clinical notes.

Doesn't my practice software already have a waitlist?

Most have a list, but staff still have to ring or text people one by one, and book whoever answers. Firsttap does the whole loop: it offers the time to the right people, books the first to tap straight into your diary, and tells everyone else it's gone.

Can each practitioner work their own way?

Yes. Each practitioner can choose automatic refills, "ask me first", or no refills, and set their own preferences.
