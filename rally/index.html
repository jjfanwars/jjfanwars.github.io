RALLY

Co-op rally. The driver can't see far. Only the navigator has the map. Talk to each other.

‹ jjfanwars
Controls

Driver W throttle · S brake / reverse · A D steer · Space handbrake · R recover (+5 s) · V chase cam · G ghost · Esc pause · mouse looks · aim the centre dot at a dash switch and click it (wipers, headlights, spot lights)

Navigator hold right mouse or Tab for the map · backup callouts: A / D then 1-6 (1 = hairpin, 6 = flat), J jump, C crest, L log, K keep middle, F flat out, B brake

Gamepad left stick steers · RT throttle · LT brake · A handbrake · Y recover

Backspace restarts the stage (solo or host)
