welcome

A warm, welcoming space for families on the Surf Coast

The Hub is a calm, inclusive place in Jan Juc where children, individuals, couples and families can find holistic support and a sense of community, from pregnancy and the early weeks with a newborn through childhood and beyond.

Everyone is welcome, whether or not you are pregnant or a parent.

how we can help
