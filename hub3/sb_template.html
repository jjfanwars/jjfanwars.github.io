Facts and assumptions

  • The Hub's shopfront is drawn from the photo on surfcoastfamilyhub.com.au: teal walls, two big windows with the tree-and-door logo, a glazed door between them, the fascia line "Nurturing perinatal, child & family wellbeing", and an A-frame sign. Proportions are approximate.
  • Address: Stuart Avenue, Jan Juc (from the Visit Great Ocean Road listing). The walk from the clifftop to Stuart Avenue is compressed into one continuous shot; the real distance is longer.
  • The neighbouring shops, houses and street trees are invented. The interior is invented: no photos of it are public.
  • The music group stands in for the Hub's Lulla-Baby Music Group. Who leads it, the ukulele and the room layout are assumptions.
  • The people are generic, simple figures, not real staff or clients.
  • Ochre sandstone cliffs, coastal heath and pigface flowers are typical of the Jan Juc clifftop; the exact view is not a real survey.
