    How it works when it’s live

    1. A client cancels by text link, on this site, or Adi cancels it in Halaxy.
    2. Halaxy tells the hub straight away (a webhook), so nobody has to notice.
    3. The hub texts the best-matched people on the waitlist: right practitioner, right day and time, longest waiting first.
    4. The first tap books them into Halaxy. Everyone else gets a polite “it’s gone” text.
    5. No taker in time? The next people get it. Too close to the time? It goes back on the online booking page.
