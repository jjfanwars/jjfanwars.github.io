Privacy

Your privacy and your information

How the Hub collects, uses and protects your personal and health information when you book, join the waitlist or see one of our practitioners.

Draft For the Hub to review before it goes live. This isn’t legal advice.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

Surf Coast Family Hub is a group of independent health practitioners at 16 Princes Tce, Jan Juc VIC 3228. Each practitioner runs their own practice and keeps their own clinical records. The Hub provides the shared front desk, booking pages and waitlist. We handle your information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and its Australian Privacy Principles, and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic).

What we collect

  • To book you in: your name, mobile, email, the practitioner, time and type of appointment (in person, video or phone), whether you have a Mental Health Treatment Plan, and that you agreed to our cancellation policy. For a child’s appointment, the child’s first name.
  • Referrals and plans: if a GP or other health professional refers you, the referral they send (your contact details, date of birth, the reason for referral and any plan). If you upload your Mental Health Treatment Plan, a copy of it. These go to your practitioner.
  • Card details (new clients): if we ask for a card to hold a booking, it goes straight to our payment provider. The Hub never sees or stores your card number.
  • For the waitlist: your name, mobile, and which practitioners, days and times suit you.
  • For your care: your practitioner records the health information they need to look after you, such as referrals, treatment plans and session notes. Only your practitioner and staff who need it can see this.

Health information is sensitive information. We only collect it with your consent, or where the law allows it.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you about and change your appointments.
  • To text you a reminder before each appointment, with links to confirm or tell us you can’t make it.
  • To text you when an earlier spot opens, if you’ve joined the waitlist, and to check in after a month to see if you still need a spot.
  • If you were referred, to text you a link to book, and to let your referrer know you’ve booked.
  • To provide your care, and to process Medicare and health fund claims.
  • To run the Hub, for example counting how many cancelled spots were refilled.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for marketing without your permission.

Who we share it with

  • Your practitioner, and Hub admin staff who need it to manage bookings.
  • The practice software your practitioner uses (Halaxy or Cliniko, which also runs video sessions), our text message provider, our payment provider, and our website host. They handle your information on our behalf and under their own privacy obligations.
  • Medicare, health funds, your GP or referrer, when this is part of your care or you ask us to.
  • Anyone else only with your consent, or where the law requires it (for example, to prevent a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety).

Information stored or handled overseas

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Australia. Before using one, we take reasonable steps to make sure it protects your information to Australian standards. Our admin assistant tool (Claude, made by Anthropic) only ever receives de-identified information: no names, phone numbers, email addresses or notes.

Text messages

We text you about your bookings and reminders, and, if you’re on the waitlist, about spots that open up. Our texts never include clinical details or the reason for your visit. Reply STOP to any waitlist text to leave the list.

Decisions made automatically

Our booking system makes some decisions without a person checking each one:

  • Who is offered a cancelled spot. When a time opens, the system texts people on the waitlist whose chosen practitioner, days, times and session length fit, starting with whoever has waited longest. The first person to tap the link gets it.
  • Pausing your waitlist place. If you don’t reply to a “still keen?” text within a week, we stop sending you offers until you tell us you’re still keen.

It uses only the booking details above, never your health information, and it never decides anything about your care or treatment. If you think one of these decisions was wrong, contact us and a person will review it.

Keeping it safe, and for how long

We protect your information with access controls, secure systems and staff training. Practitioners keep health records for as long as the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) requires: generally at least seven years after your last visit, or until a child turns 25. We delete your waitlist details when you leave the list or are booked in from it. Online referrals and uploaded plans are kept only until they’re in your practitioner’s records. If there’s ever a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by contacting us or your practitioner. We’ll respond within 30 days. In rare cases the law lets us refuse, and if so we’ll explain why.
