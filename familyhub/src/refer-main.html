Referrals

Send a referral online

For GPs, midwives, paediatricians and other health professionals. It takes about two minutes, and your patient gets a text with a link to book straight away. You can still email or fax if you prefer.

Demo Referrals are pretend and stay in this browser. Open the Hub dashboard to see it arrive.
