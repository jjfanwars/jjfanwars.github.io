    How it works when it’s live

    1. Two days before, every client gets a reminder with “confirm” and “can’t make it” links. Can’t-make-its free the time early, while it can still be filled.
    2. A client cancels by text link, on this site, or staff cancel it in Halaxy. Halaxy tells the hub straight away (a webhook).
    3. The hub works out how long the gap is, then texts the best-matched people on the waitlist: right practitioner, day, time and session length, longest waiting first.
    4. The first tap books them into Halaxy. Everyone else gets a polite “it’s gone” text. If they already had a later booking, that time goes to the next person.
    5. No taker in time? The next people get it. Too close to the time? It goes back on the online booking page.
    6. After a month on the list, people get a “still keen?” text, so offers only go to people who still want a spot.
