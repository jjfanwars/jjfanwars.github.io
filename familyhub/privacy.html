Privacy

Your privacy and your information

How the Hub collects, uses and protects your personal and health information when you book, join the waitlist or see one of our practitioners.

Draft For the Hub to review before it goes live. This isn’t legal advice.

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Surf Coast Family Hub is a group of independent health practitioners at 16 Princes Tce, Jan Juc VIC 3228. Each practitioner runs their own practice and keeps their own clinical records. The Hub provides the shared front desk, booking pages and waitlist. We handle your information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and its Australian Privacy Principles, and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic).

What we collect

  • To book you in: your name, mobile, email, the practitioner, time and type of appointment, and whether you have a Mental Health Treatment Plan. For a child’s appointment, the child’s first name.
  • For the waitlist: your name, mobile, and which practitioners, days and times suit you.
  • For your care: your practitioner records the health information they need to look after you, such as referrals, treatment plans and session notes. Only your practitioner and staff who need it can see this.

Health information is sensitive information. We only collect it with your consent, or where the law allows it.

How we use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you about and change your appointments.
  • To text you when an earlier spot opens, if you’ve joined the waitlist.
  • To provide your care, and to process Medicare and health fund claims.
  • To run the Hub, for example counting how many cancelled spots were refilled.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for marketing without your permission.

Who we share it with

  • Your practitioner, and Hub admin staff who need it to manage bookings.
  • The practice software your practitioner uses (Halaxy or Cliniko), our text message provider, and our website host. They handle your information on our behalf and under their own privacy obligations.
  • Medicare, health funds, your GP or referrer, when this is part of your care or you ask us to.
  • Anyone else only with your consent, or where the law requires it (for example, to prevent a serious threat to someone’s life, health or safety).

Information stored or handled overseas

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Australia. Before using one, we take reasonable steps to make sure it protects your information to Australian standards. Our admin assistant tool (Claude, made by Anthropic) only ever receives de-identified information: no names, phone numbers, email addresses or notes.

Text messages

We text you about your bookings and, if you’re on the waitlist, about spots that open up. Our texts never include clinical details. Reply STOP to any waitlist text to leave the list.

Keeping it safe, and for how long

We protect your information with access controls, secure systems and staff training. Practitioners keep health records for as long as the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) requires: generally at least seven years after your last visit, or until a child turns 25. We delete your waitlist details when you leave the list or are booked in from it. If there’s ever a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by contacting us or your practitioner. We’ll respond within 30 days. In rare cases the law lets us refuse, and if so we’ll explain why.

Questions or complaints

Contact us first on 0491 184 788 or hello@surfcoastfamilyhub.com.au. We’ll try to resolve it within 30 days. If you’re not happy with our response, you can contact:

  • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992
  • the Health Complaints Commissioner (Victoria): hcc.vic.gov.au, 1300 582 113
  • for concerns about a registered practitioner’s conduct, Ahpra: ahpra.gov.au, 1300 419 495
