HOW TO FISH

  1. Buy a fishing rod at the harbour shed, then press 2 to take it out.
  2. Face open water: off the boat's deck, the jetty, a dock or a beach. Hold left mouse to wind up and let go to cast. The longer you hold, the further it flies.
  3. Wait for a bite. When the bobber goes under and you see !, click quickly to set the hook.
  4. Reel it in: hold left mouse to reel, let go to ease off. Keep the needle in the green. Too much tension snaps the line; too little and the fish slips off. Big fish surge, so ease off when the needle jumps.
  5. Fish go in your cooler. Sell them at the harbour shed.

Where to fish: near islands for sea bass, deep water far from land for cod and tuna, around wrecks and the rig for eels. Storms bring up stranger things. Better rods mean quicker bites, easier fights and rarer catches. Press H any time for this card.
