Stop 1 · Bells Beach

Hello, and welcome

The walk starts where the big swells come in. So does ours: we're a family hub run by locals, for anyone raising little people on this bit of coast.

New to town, new to parenting, or just after some company on a grey Tuesday? Come in. The kettle's usually on.

sandy feet, prams and muddy dogs all fine ☺

Stop 2 · Winkipop

Who we are

Parents, grandparents, early-childhood workers and a few volunteers who make a very good scone. No forms to fill in at the door, no judgement, no rush.

We keep things small and gentle, so kids get to know each other and grown-ups get to know their neighbours.
