Welcome

A home base for Surf Coast families

Sandy feet welcome. We're a friendly, local place where parents, carers and kids can connect, play and find support, from the very first weeks with a newborn to the busy school years.

Pop in for a cuppa, join a playgroup, or just come and say hi. There's always a warm spot on the couch.

Programs

Something for every season

Gentle, play-based and run by people who love this little corner of the coast.

Messy play, songs and sandpit fun for 0–5s, with a cuppa for the grown-ups.

Parent & Bub

A soft landing for new parents. Share the wins, the wobbles and the sleepless nights.

Tuesday mornings

Rockpool Rangers

Nature play on the coast: rockpools, beach treasures and bush walks for curious kids.
