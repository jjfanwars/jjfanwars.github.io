James Racing

Realistic single-seater racing from the cockpit. Downforce, tyre temperatures, ERS, DRS, eight gears, and nine rivals who brake late.

Controls

W / ↑ throttle · S / ↓ brake (hold at a stop for reverse) · A D / ← → steer · E / Shift gear up · Q / Ctrl gear down · Space DRS · X ERS mode (balanced / overtake / harvest) · V camera · R back on track · Esc pause

Gamepad: left stick steers, right trigger throttle, left trigger brake, RB / A up, LB / X down, Y DRS, B camera. Wheels that show up as a gamepad work too.

DRS opens in the zone on the main straight: in a race only from lap 3, and only when you were within a second of the car ahead at the detection line. Tyres grip best around 100 °C. Brakes can lock with ABS off. Four wheels over the white line is a track-limits warning (three, then 5 s penalties).
